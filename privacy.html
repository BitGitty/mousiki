Privacy policy — Mousiki Uploader

Last updated: 8 October 2026

"Mousiki Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Mousiki (@MousikiK) to upload and manage that channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

  • It accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel, with the owner's permission.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of other people.
  • Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
